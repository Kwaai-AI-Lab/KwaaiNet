Two withdrawn drafts, and what replaced them

Asked whether vessels are prominent in the District Six memoir, they are not. Share of chunks carrying vessel language:

MobyDickD6WarPeaceManhattanCountryHistory
31%1%0%0%0%

Vessel was a MobyDick type placed in the shared narrative-history module on genre reasoning, and four corpora inherited it for nothing. The larger fault: the modules were written top-down from genre priors, and D6's schema was a four-line extension of one rather than something read out of the book.

The rewrite that followed was also wrong, in the same way but smaller. It made dispossessed a central predicate on the strength of one vivid Group Areas Board passage. Dispossession language turns out to be 1.5% of the corpus: the memoir covers 1897–1956 and the demolitions began in the 1960s. Removal is the shadow the title casts, not the events the book narrates.

D6 v3 is built from scratch — from the doc schema, the 25-question eval set, a systematic 14-point sample across all 1152 chunks, and full-corpus probes. Every type carries its measured evidence share, and the types that failed are listed with their numbers so the next revision cannot re-propose them. 23 of the 25 eval questions are answerable from it.

The unverified schemas below have not had this treatment and carry the original fault. Treat their diagrams as proposals, not descriptions.
