Chunk-link ratios are near-identical between the productive and dead KBs (55%
vs 54%), so evidence coverage is not the discriminator either. What separates
them is what the documents are about: dream's relation vocabulary is person- and
organisation-centric, so narrative and biographical corpora yield relations while
scientific papers yield almost none. Climate and Astrophysics are not broken —
they are being asked for relations their text does not contain.

The implication is that "enrich every KB with dream cycles" has a natural
ceiling on technical corpora, and those KBs need either a domain-appropriate
relation schema or a different enrichment strategy entirely.
