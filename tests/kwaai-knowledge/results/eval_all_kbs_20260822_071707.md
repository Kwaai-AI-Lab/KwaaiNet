WarPeace carries 2.7x the relations of the next KB and retrieves *below the
median*. Legal has two relations and the best recall in the set. OSMDocs has
240 entities and zero relations, and still beats Astrophysics (6437 entities,
23 relations). DeepSea has zero relations and beats Astrophysics too.

This corroborates the D6 result from an independent direction: D6 gained 114
relations overnight and recall moved 0pp (89.5%, against pre-sweep baselines of
88.5% and ~90.0%). One KB before-and-after, and fifteen KBs across, agree.

### Why

Retrieval here is carried by hybrid BM25 + dense vectors. The graph contributes
little, and what dream adds contributes nothing measurable, because **every
relation extraction produces is `associated_with`** — 31 of 31 sampled across
WarPeace's major characters, with no typed relation of any kind. An untyped edge
records that two entities are related without recording how, which is not enough
for retrieval to exploit. D6's typed relations (`spouse_of`, `parent_of`) come
from the YAML seed, not extraction.

The weak positive on *entity* count (+0.40 Pearson) is more plausibly corpus
size showing through — bigger corpora have both more entities and more text to
retrieve from — than evidence that entities help.

### Caveats

n = 15 is small. At this size only the null result on relations is safe to lean
on (+0.081 is unambiguously nothing); the entity correlation is borderline and
should not be treated as established. Question sets differ in size (58–80
expected tokens) and difficulty, and WarPeace is a single document, so its
questions are not comparable to a 13-document corpus. These are baselines for
tracking change over time, not a leaderboard.

### What follows

1. **Stop treating relation count as progress.** It is not correlated with the
   outcome anyone cares about.
2. **A typed relation vocabulary is the prerequisite** for the graph to earn its
   place. Until extraction emits something other than `associated_with`, more
   cycles cannot help.
3. **Entity hygiene first.** WarPeace links Pierre to `Whomhast` and
   `Russianforces`; D6 types `Granny` and `Solly` as Organizations. Enrichment
   spreads these rather than fixing them.
4. **Meetings (47.5%) and Climate (48.4%) are the weakest** and are where
   retrieval work would pay, not the KBs with the fewest relations.
