This reversal marked a significant shift in the Court's understanding of the Constitution and its commitment to protecting the rights of marginalized communities.

The flip from Dred Scott to Plessy to Brown illustrates how the Supreme Court can evolve over time to recognize new constitutional principles and values, often in response to changing social norms and political pressures.
