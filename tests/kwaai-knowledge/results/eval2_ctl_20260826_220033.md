### q17 — What was Hewat Training College?

Hewat Training College was a teacher training college for "Coloureds" (a demographic category used in South Africa at the time) located in Cape Town. It was established on land previously used as a whites-only primary school, which was considered too close to District Six and the prison.

It served as the first Post Matriculation Teacher Training facility for Coloureds in the country, opening its doors in 1944 with considerable fanfare [4]. The college trained teachers for the upper years of the primary division [16].

The staff at Hewat College was chosen with the intention of preventing the incubation of left-wing radical ideas, and consisted of academics with a conservative educational philosophy, including those recruited from the United Kingdom [20].
