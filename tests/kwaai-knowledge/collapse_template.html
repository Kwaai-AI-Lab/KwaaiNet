What the fallback swallows

Enforcing a declared vocabulary stops the extractor inventing a predicate per sentence. It also routes everything undeclared into associated_with — and most of what goes there meant something.

The trade is real. Run 1 emitted 88 predicate types because nothing constrained it. Enforcement cut that to 26 — but of the {{NLOST}} undeclared predicates it absorbed, only 11 were noise. The other {{NLOST}} carried meaning, {{ELOST}} edges’ worth, and associated_with keeps none of it. The original predicate is discarded at write time, so the loss is irreversible.

The {{NLOST}} predicates a memoir loses

Emitted by the unenforced run, absorbed by the fallback in every enforced run. Counts shown where an edge occurred more than once.

These are not synonyms for “related to”. nursed_by, disagreed_with, resigned_under, forced_to_obey and segregated lives are distinctions a memoir of District Six is largely made of. Losing them is not tidying — it is discarding the corpus’s own account of how people stood to one another.
