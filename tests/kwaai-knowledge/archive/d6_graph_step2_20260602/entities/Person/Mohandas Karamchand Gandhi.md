Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi (Mahatma Gandhi) lived in South Africa from 1893 to 1914 and organised the Indian community's resistance to discriminatory legislation, developing his method of non-violent resistance (satyagraha). He knew J.M.H. Gool personally, visited the Gool family mansion at 7 Buitencingle Street, and was a subscriber to the Indian Opinion. His connection to the Gool family is documented in archival correspondence.
