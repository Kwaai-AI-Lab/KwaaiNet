Abdul Hamid Gool (known as B.G. or Cheops) was a son of Haji Joosub Maulvi Hamid Gool and his second wife Wahida. He studied medicine in London and was known in Cape Town for his commanding personality and pharaonic bearing. He married Cissie Gool (Zainunnissa), daughter of Dr. Abdullah Abdurahman. He was a doctor and prominent figure in the extended Gool family circle.
