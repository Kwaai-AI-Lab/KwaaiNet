Haji Joosub Maulvi Hamid Gool was the author's maternal grandfather, a prosperous merchant from India who arrived at the Cape via Mauritius in 1884. His father was a Pushto-speaking Maulvi from the Province of Swat who had moved to Rander in Gujarat. Joosub left behind his eleven-year-old bride in India and came to Cape Town where he established J.M.H. Gool & Co. at 25 Church Street, supplying spices and becoming a supplier to the troops of Queen Victoria during the Anglo-Boer War. He founded the Hanaffi Quwatul Islam Mosque in Loop Street (completed 1898), built the grand 13-room mansion at 7 Buitencingle Street, and knew Gandhi, Cecil Rhodes, and Dr. Abdullah Abdurahman personally.
